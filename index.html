  • Counter Clock

  • Well-Tempered Clock

    A clock where sunrise, noon, sunset, and midnight always land on 6, 12, 6, and 12.

  • 24 Hour Clock

    A clock where the hour hand sweeps the full face once a day, so noon points straight down.

  • Month Clock

    The hour hand tracks the month, the minute hand tracks the day of the month, the second hand tracks the hour of the day (12-hour cycle), and a fourth hand tracks the seconds.

  • Second Clock

    A single hand counts the seconds of the day, from 0 to 86,400, so noon lands at the bottom.
