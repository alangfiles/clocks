A clock based on your location. Well-Tempered Time is a wonderful new system where there are 12 hours of sunlight every day! Midnight is in the middle of the night, and noon is in the middle of the day. All of this beautiful time symmetry comes at the low price of "second variability" aka not all seconds are created equal. A small price to pay for such benefits!

Even a broken clock is right twice a day...

Because Well-Tempered Time actually moves forward (at two different rates), there's not always matches unless the imperial time has sunrise or sunset before 6am/pm.
